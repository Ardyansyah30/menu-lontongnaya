## 🎬 Animasi Terbaru yang Digunakan

1. **Framer Motion Spring Animation** - Smooth, natural feeling
2. **Stagger Animation** - Elements appear one by one
3. **Hover Scale & Y-axis** - Professional lift effect
4. **Gradient Background Animation** - Dynamic color shifts
5. **Shine Effect** - Luxury brand feel
6. **Floating Animation** - Floating objects
7. **Rotate Animation** - Spinning decorative elements
